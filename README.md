I'm interested in how chemical understanding, reliable data, and machine learning can work together to guide experiments. My work connects organic synthesis with reaction-data curation, molecular representations, and computational workflows.

Currently, I am studying **enantioselectivity in nickel-catalyzed asymmetric coupling** and contributing to tools for automated chemistry. I previously conducted undergraduate research in the **Toste group at UC Berkeley**, working on photochemical [2+2] cycloadditions and asymmetric sulfur(IV) oxidation.

### Featured project

#### [NiAsymCoupling](https://github.com/EthanTang2005/NiAsymCoupling)

A curated collection of literature-derived nickel asymmetric coupling results, linking molecular structures, reaction conditions, and selectivity to their sources.

- **7,935 source-result rows**, including **4,596 main-scope rows** for nickel-reported C(sp²)–C(sp³) bond formation.
- SQLite and CSV data with source locators, explicit missing values, and quality flags.
- Query examples, validation scripts, and documentation for reproducible use.

These counts describe source-result rows, not independent experiments. See the repository for scope, data access, licensing, and known limitations.

### Research in progress

**Learning selectivity from limited reaction data**  
Comparing molecular descriptors, fingerprints, and pretrained 3D representations for nickel-catalyzed asymmetric coupling, with attention to ligand-grouped evaluation and generalization across literature sources.

**Chemical inputs and literature data for OptoClaw**  
My contributions focus on generating initial 3D molecular conformers and building a paper title/abstract database, including DOI reconciliation and provenance tracking.

**Connecting computation with experiment**  
Developing nickel-complex construction and structural-screening workflows alongside my background in synthesis and reaction analysis.
